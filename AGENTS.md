# Documentation policy

- Keep `README.md` and `README-EN.md` focused on generic OS/advertising-SDK support and usage.
- Do not add names or real bundle IDs of apps being adapted/supported, or their app-specific device-verification results, to either README.
- This restriction does not apply to intentionally named explanatory examples, such as YouTube in the out-of-scope embedded-ad explanation. Preserve those examples.
- Do not add an “実機での動作確認” / “Confirmed on device” section to either README.
- If app-specific investigation details are needed, keep them in technical documentation or test notes, not the READMEs.
- Keep the Japanese and English READMEs consistent when updating them.
